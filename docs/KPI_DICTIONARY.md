# KPI Dictionary

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| active portfolio | Outstanding active credit portfolio | Sum of active portfolio balance | portfolio_kbob | not applicable | thousand BOB | monthly | branch, global | Measures scale of credit activity | Does not measure welfare impact | data_dictionary; kpi_branch_summary | Executive overview | Non-negative for observed analytical rows |
