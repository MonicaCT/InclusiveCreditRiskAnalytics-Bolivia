# Stakeholder Requirements

| stakeholder | decision | information need | KPI | visual | frequency | level of detail | risk of misinterpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive management | Whether portfolio growth is responsible and sustainable | Global portfolio, clients, delinquency, inclusion score and stress flags | active portfolio; clients; delinquency; responsible inclusion score | executive KPI cards and stress summary | monthly or quarterly | global and branch summary | Treating growth alone as success without risk context |
| Branch managers | Which branch requires operational attention | Branch portfolio, clients, average balance, mora and growth | branch share; active portfolio; clients; delinquency | branch performance table and risk-growth matrix | monthly | branch-level | Ranking branches without considering maturity stage |
| Risk team | Whether delinquency or stress scenarios require action | Mora, max mora, stressed mora, risk flag and forecast gap | delinquency; stress-test outcomes; forecast accuracy | mora monitor and stress-test table | monthly | branch and scenario | Treating stress scenarios as predictions rather than governance tools |
| Financial-inclusion team | Whether outreach is broadening access responsibly | Clients, clients per million BOB, territorial balance and average balance | clients; territorial balance; responsible inclusion score | inclusion and territorial balance charts | monthly/quarterly | branch and global | Treating branch-level outreach as direct poverty impact |
| Data team | Whether public outputs are traceable and safe | Source separation, field definitions, validation status and privacy boundary | data-quality checks; missingness and domain checks | data-quality report and SQL validation | per release | dataset/table level | Publishing raw or identifiable fields by accident |
| External reviewer | Whether results are reproducible and proportionate | Methodology, limitations, validation, source notes and public outputs | forecast accuracy; quality checks; KPI dictionary | methodology, executive tables and validation report | per review | project-level | Overstating causal claims or tool maturity |
