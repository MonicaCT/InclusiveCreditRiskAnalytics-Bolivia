# Dashboard specification

## Executive Overview

Cards: Total Portfolio, Total Clients, Delinquency Rate, Portfolio Growth MoM, Average Balance, Responsible Inclusion Score.

## Growth and Inclusion

Visuals: portfolio evolution, client growth, average balance and territorial balance score.

## Portfolio Quality

Visuals: delinquency trend, growth-risk positioning, concentration and alert flags.

## Branch Performance

Visuals: branch comparison, portfolio share, client share, growth and risk flag.

## Forecasting

Visuals: observed versus forecast portfolio, prediction intervals, model comparison and validation error.

## Stress Testing

Visuals: base, moderate and adverse scenarios, portfolio effect, delinquency effect and recommended action.

All pages must use public processed outputs only.
