# Page Specifications

## 1. Executive Overview

Purpose: show the overall portfolio, outreach, delinquency and responsible inclusion story in one page.

Visuals:

- KPI cards: Total Portfolio, Total Clients, Delinquency Rate, Responsible Inclusion Score.
- Line chart: Total Portfolio by date.
- Line chart: Total Clients by date.
- Bar chart: branch portfolio share.
- Narrative card: KPI Status Label.

## 2. Growth and Inclusion

Purpose: connect portfolio expansion with client outreach and inclusion quality.

Visuals:

- Line chart: portfolio and clients over time.
- Scatter or combo chart: client outreach index vs portfolio depth index.
- KPI cards: Territorial Client Balance and Territorial Portfolio Balance.
- Branch slicer.

## 3. Portfolio Quality

Purpose: monitor mora, overdue portfolio and risk penalty.

Visuals:

- Line chart: Delinquency Rate.
- Column chart: Overdue Portfolio by branch.
- KPI cards: Risk Penalty and Portfolio Quality Score.
- Status label: Risk Status Label.

## 4. Branch Performance

Purpose: compare Coroico, Irupana and Global views without double counting.

Visuals:

- Matrix: branch, portfolio, clients, branch shares and responsible inclusion score.
- Bar chart: branch portfolio share.
- Bar chart: branch client share.
- Small multiples: branch portfolio trend.

## 5. Forecasting

Purpose: communicate forecasted portfolio paths and model uncertainty.

Visuals:

- Line and ribbon: Forecast Portfolio, 80 and 95 percent intervals.
- KPI cards: Forecast RMSE, MAE and MAPE.
- Slicer: forecast model.
- Text card: Selected Forecast Model.

## 6. Stress Testing

Purpose: compare stress scenarios by portfolio, clients, mora and risk-weighted growth.

Visuals:

- Scenario slicer.
- Bar chart: Stressed Portfolio by branch.
- KPI cards: Stressed Delinquency Rate, Risk Weighted Growth and Stressed Clients.
- Table: scenario, risk flag and development read.
