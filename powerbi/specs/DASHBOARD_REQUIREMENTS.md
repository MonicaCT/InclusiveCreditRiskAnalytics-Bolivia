# Dashboard Requirements

## Objective

Create an executive Power BI report that communicates portfolio growth, responsible financial inclusion, risk monitoring, forecast uncertainty and stress-test exposure using public aggregate data only.

## Users

- Recruiters evaluating data analytics and BI capability.
- Development-finance stakeholders.
- Portfolio monitoring teams.
- Research and policy audiences.

## Required Pages

1. Executive Overview.
2. Growth and Inclusion.
3. Portfolio Quality.
4. Branch Performance.
5. Forecasting.
6. Stress Testing.

## Required Capabilities

- KPI cards for portfolio, clients, delinquency and responsible inclusion.
- Branch and date filtering.
- Model filtering for forecasts.
- Scenario filtering for stress testing.
- Tooltips that explain units and limitations.
- Notes that results are monitoring indicators, not causal poverty estimates.

## Non-Requirements

- No borrower-level drill-through.
- No raw workbook import.
- No unapproved publication of private operational data.
- No causal claim about poverty reduction.
